## Patterns worth borrowing

1. **App shell: labelled sidebar + content sheet** (Delphi 01, Sprig 04, Linktree 03). Sidebar 15 to 17% of viewport (about 240 to 280 CSS px), icon + label rows, active item as a grey pill, account at the bottom, one primary action button above it ("Run test"). *Phone:* sidebar becomes a 5-item bottom tab bar (Dashboard, Test, History, Assistant, More); the primary action becomes a centre tab or floating button; secondary items move under "More".

2. **Dashboard: hero metric card, then a 2-column grid** (Delphi 01). Full-width tinted card with label + period dropdown, the number directly under it, then the chart; below, two equal cards with a nested white card inside a grey tile. Keep the content column narrow (about 57% of the viewport) rather than stretching edge to edge. *Phone:* single column, hero card first, chart height reduced to about 160 px, the two cards stack.

3. **Stat row as one divided card** (Ferndesk 02; Linktree 03 and Sprig 04 use separate cards). 3 to 5 cells: icon, numeral, label, one grey explanatory sentence. For download / upload / latency / percent of plan. *Phone:* 2x2 grid, or a horizontal scroll row with the next card peeking; drop the explanatory sentence to a tap-through.

4. **AI insight block marked by a sparkle icon, a chip and a provenance line** (Sprig 04, ClickUp 05). Tinted sparkle icon + "AI Insights" heading, "Summary" chip, plain paragraph, then "Generated on [date]". Add "based on N tests" for grounding. Place it top-left of the dashboard spanning half the width, as ClickUp does. *Phone:* full-width card placed directly under the hero metric, text clamped to 4 lines with "Read more".

5. **Per-card "ask AI" chips opening a right-hand assistant panel** (Linktree 03). Each chart card ends with a pill "sparkle + suggested question". The panel is about 26% of the viewport, floats as a rounded card, shows the steps it took ("Fetching your tests") and then a structured answer. *Phone:* chips stay on the cards; the panel becomes a full-screen sheet sliding up, with a close button and the composer pinned above the keyboard.

6. **Assistant page: centred conversation + evidence panel** (ChatGPT 06). Conversation text column about 815 px inside a wider area; right panel with a segmented control ("Activity | Sources") listing which data was read; disclaimer under the composer. For us the tabs would be "Your data used | Sources". *Phone:* conversation goes full width; the evidence panel collapses into a "N sources" chip under each answer that opens a bottom sheet; chat history moves into a drawer.

7. **Diagnosis: score + factors, evidence beside it** (Visitors 07, Square 08). Ring with the number, verdict word, one sentence and threshold legend on the left; on the right a factor list where each row is name + status pill + one sentence, starting with an honest caveat and ending with "Last updated". Main column repeats each factor as a card with its chart and bulleted evidence carrying the same pill. Add a confidence pill in the same style. *Phone:* ring and verdict first, centred; factor list becomes an accordion directly below; evidence cards follow in the same order; the right rail no longer exists.

8. **Plan comparison with a highlighted column** (Asana 09). Label column + plan columns; the recommended one has a coloured cap, a tint and a border the full table height, and the only filled button. For us: "Your plan" vs. "What you measure" vs. alternatives. *Phone:* do not shrink the table. Show one plan per card in a horizontal swipe with the recommended card first, or keep the label column sticky and scroll the plan columns sideways.

9. **Report review: document preview + AI side panel + action bar** (Dovetail 10, Sprig 04 header). Page-shaped preview centred; right panel with AI-drafted sections and citation chips linking to the data; top-right Edit / Share / Export buttons; metadata as a definition list. Put the "Approve and send" button here and nowhere else. *Phone:* preview takes the full width; the AI panel becomes a tab ("Report | AI notes"); Approve and Export move into a sticky bottom bar.

10. **Onboarding: left step rail + centred card** (Mercury 11) and **settings: stacked cards with worded toggles** (KAYAK 12). Onboarding has a top progress bar, an "n / total" counter, a checked step list, and the consent checkbox just above the Continue button. Settings cards each have a title, one explanatory paragraph, and rows with a toggle plus "On/Off" text; delete lives alone at the bottom. *Phone:* step rail collapses to the top progress bar plus "Step 2 of 4"; card becomes full-bleed; buttons become full-width and sticky at the bottom. Settings cards go full width; the toggle stays on the right of each row; the settings sub-navigation becomes a list screen that drills in.

**Landing page recipe** (incident.io 13, Givingli 14, Miro 15, Wise 16): centred hero with a two-tone headline, two buttons, and a desktop frame with an overlapping phone frame; a flat tinted "How it works" band with three numbered columns; an AI section of two cards each pairing a cropped UI fragment with a headline; a colour-inverted privacy band with three icon points. *Phone:* headline drops to about 40 px, buttons stack full width, the hero shows the phone frame only; three columns stack vertically with numbers left-aligned; AI cards stack; the top nav becomes a logo + menu button + one CTA.
