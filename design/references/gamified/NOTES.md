## Patterns worth borrowing

1. **Score ring (Home)** — from WHOOP + Bevel. Full 360 degree ring, about 65% of screen width, stroke 8 to 10% of diameter, start at 12 o'clock, clockwise. Center stack: tiny all-caps label ("OF PLAN SPEED"), number 60+ pt heavy, "%" at about 60% size. One flat status color for the whole fill rather than a rainbow; track is a dim neutral.
2. **Status color scale** — from WHOOP / Oura / One. Three bands only: good green (~#2DBE2D to #5FE0B7), warning yellow (~#FFDE00), bad coral-red (~#FF5A5F). Tie the bands to our 80% line (at or above 80% = green). Always pair the color with a status WORD next to the number ("FAIR", "Good"), as Oura and Lloyds do, so color is never the only signal.
3. **One-line verdict under the ring + AI entry** — from WHOOP and Bevel. Bold short verdict ("Solid Recovery") then 2 to 3 lines of plain explanation, then either an outlined "Chat to learn about..." pill (WHOOP) or a glow-bordered AI insight card with emoji headline (Bevel). This is where our AI feature should live on Home.
4. **14-day tracker** — from Alta + Tonal + Chipotle. Two rows of 7 dots (about 20 pt, 8 pt gap), filled accent + check for completed days, darker tint for older days, flat grey for upcoming, day letter above with today tinted. Put "Day 6 of 14" and "22 of 56 tests" as text beside it. Alternative for the Home card: Chipotle's segmented arch with 14 blocks and a sparkle on day 7 and day 14.
5. **Live speed test gauge** — baseline is Starlink (270 degree thin arc, non-linear tick labels, needle, readout below). To beat it: keep the 270 degree arc and non-linear scale, but thicken the stroke to about 8%, add a track, put the live Mbps number INSIDE the arc (as Lloyds / One do), and add a marker knob on the arc at the plan's "typical speed" (Lloyds hollow-ring knob) so the user sees measured vs. label at a glance.
6. **History daily bars with 80% threshold line** — from BitePal + Yazio + Garmin. 14 rounded-top bars; a dotted or thin solid horizontal line across the full plot at 80% with a small label at its right end; y-axis labels on the right. Color per bar by status as Garmin does (met = green, below = warning color), and optionally a grey ghost bar up to 100% of plan so the shortfall reads visually.
7. **Worst-day callout** — from Opal. Sentence headline with colored keywords above the chart ("Your slowest day was Monday at 41% of plan"), with the offending bar in a white-to-red gradient and a value label above each bar.
8. **Contributors list (why the score is what it is)** — from Oura + Quo. Rows of label, right-aligned value or status word, chevron, and a 3 pt progress line under each; the line and word turn orange with "Pay attention" when bad. Use for download, upload, latency, evening-hours performance.
9. **Verdict reveal** — from Noom + Runna + Opal. Story-style segmented progress dashes at top, dark screen with a spotlight cone, verdict sentence in large type ("You get 78% of the speed you pay for") with the number colored, gauge or ring animating in, and a Share pill inside the card itself.
10. **Completion reward** — from Runna + Duolingo. Hexagon badge with beveled layers and the key number inside, confetti limited to the top 40% of the screen, date chip under the badge (Duolingo "DEC 6, 2025" tag), one primary button. Keep it small and single-step.
