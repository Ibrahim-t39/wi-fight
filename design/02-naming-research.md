# App name research

Quick web check only, not a trademark clearance. One or two searches per name. USPTO, the app stores, and domains were not checked.

About 50 candidates were generated, cut to 12, and collision-checked.

## Top 5, ranked

| # | Name | Why | Tagline | Collision risk | Logo idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Proofband** | "Proof" plus "broadband". Says the positioning line in one word. | Proof of the speed you pay for. | Low. Only cover bands and unrelated "Proof" apps turned up. | A checkmark whose long stroke sweeps up into a speed-gauge arc |
| 2 | **Parline** | "Par" is the standard you are owed. The line is your connection. | Is your internet up to par? | Low. One dissolved UK holding company. Sounds a little like Parler and Parlem Telecom. | A horizontal par line with one bar rising just short of it |
| 3 | **AsPaid** | Short for "as paid for". Reads like the verdict. | Get the speed you paid for. | Low to medium. Near payment apps AsPay and SayPaid. | A receipt with a torn edge shaped like a waveform |
| 4 | **Fairband** | Fair plus broadband. Clearest fit with SDG 9.c. | Fair speed. Fair price. With proof. | Medium. Feedzai Fairband (an AI-fairness algorithm), Fairband Financial Group, a Bangladesh ISP app. | A balance scale whose pans are Wi-Fi arcs |
| 5 | **PaidFor** | Lifts the key phrase from the positioning line. | The speed you paid for, proven. | Low to medium. Generic phrase, hard to search and protect. | A price tag whose hole is a gauge dial |

## Shortlisted but set aside

- **Trueline:** high. A former US broadband builder and an existing app.
- **Vouch:** high. Well-known insurance fintech.
- **Attest:** high. Funded consumer research company.
- **Plumb:** medium to high. Too close to Plum, the UK money app.
- **Labelwise:** high. Several label-scanner apps already use it.
- **Squarely:** medium to high. Multiple software companies.
- **NetVerdict:** low on exact match, but sounds like a security scanner.

## Decision

The team chose **Wi-Fight**, the working name from the original Goal 9 proposal. A quick web search for "Wi-Fight" and "WiFight" found no existing app with that name. This was one search, not a trademark check. The AI feature keeps the name **Proof AI**.
