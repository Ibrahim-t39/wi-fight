# Design direction v1: Wi-Fight

Name: **Wi-Fight** (chosen by the team; it was the working name in the original Goal 9 proposal). AI feature name: **Proof AI**. The product is a responsive website that works on a desktop browser and on a phone.

## Principles

1. **Light app, dark moments.** Everyday pages are light so they read on a projector. The live test, the verdict reveal, and the share card go dark and immersive.
2. **One brand color.** Cobalt `#2747F5`. Green, amber, and coral are reserved for status and always come with a word.
3. **One AI marker.** A thin cobalt-violet-mint gradient border plus the "Proof AI" label. It appears only on AI content, nowhere else.
4. **AI shows its work.** Every AI block has: a plain sentence, numbered reasons, evidence chips (test count, days, confidence, method), a freshness stamp, and a one-line "generated by AI" note.
5. **The user approves.** Anything AI drafts has Approve, Edit, and Regenerate. Nothing is sent without approval.
6. **Big number, small label.** One hero number per page.
7. **Plain words.** "Fair line" instead of threshold. "Response time" instead of latency. "Provider" instead of ISP.

## Layout

- Desktop (over 1100px): 256px sidebar, 12-column grid, content max 1240px.
- Tablet (720 to 1100px): sidebar collapses to an 84px icon rail, cards go full width.
- Phone (under 720px): no sidebar, floating glass tab bar with a round "Run a test" button in the center, everything stacks.
