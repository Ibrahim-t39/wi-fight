03PROOF AI

AI that shows its work.

Proof AI turns your test results into something you can act on, and it always puts the evidence next to the words.

It works out whether the cause is your provider or your home Wi-Fi.

Each piece of evidence moves a score you can see, so you can check the conclusion yourself.

It answers your questions

How it is kept honest

  • The app's own code does the math, and the model only explains it.
  • Every number the model writes is checked against your real data.
  • Each answer names the model that wrote it.
  • One switch on the Privacy and data page turns it off.
04Cybersecurity

Security you can see for yourself.

  • Consent comes first. Nothing runs until you have read what is collected and agreed to it.
  • Your data is encrypted on your device. It is locked with AES-GCM 256 before it is saved.
  • There is no password. You sign in with a one-time code or a passkey, so there is nothing to steal or reuse.
  • Nothing is sent without your approval. The send button stays locked until you approve the report.
  • Every report carries a fingerprint. If a number is changed afterward, the SHA-256 fingerprint no longer matches.

05Why it matters

Affordable internet only counts if you get what you pay for.

The cables are being built, and in a city like Huntsville most homes can already get a connection. The open question is whether the speed that reaches the home matches the bill.

Target 9.c

Universal and affordable access to the internet. A plan is not truly affordable when part of the bill buys speed that never arrives.

Target 9.1

Reliable infrastructure with affordable, fair access. The networks people rely on should work as promised for everyone who pays for them.
