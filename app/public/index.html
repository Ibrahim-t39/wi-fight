The broadband label

Promised vs. what you got

Wi-Fight puts your provider's promise and your own results on one card.

Sample data
1

What the label is

Every provider has to show a Broadband Facts label for each plan. It works like a nutrition label for internet: price and speeds in one fixed layout.

2

What "typical speed" means

It is the speed the provider says most customers on the plan usually get. It is their promise, not a top speed.

3

What "You got" adds

The label only shows the promise. Wi-Fight adds your own measured middle value next to it, so you can see the gap.

One thing to know before you start

Speed tests run on M-Lab, an open research platform. M-Lab publishes test results with the IP address. We tell you before your first test.

Encrypted on your device

Your results are locked with AES-GCM encryption in your browser. Nothing leaves it unless you approve.

Sign in with an email code or passkey

No password to remember, reuse, or leak.
