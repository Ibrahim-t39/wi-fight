1A few minutes

Set it up once

You agree to what is collected, sign in without a password, and add your plan, either from a list or by letting Proof AI read a photo of your bill.

2Two weeks

Wi-Fight tests on its own

It runs short tests a day at different times, and marks each day as on plan or below the fair line.

3Your decision

You get a verdict and a report

Wi-Fight tells you plainly whether you got what you paid for, and Proof AI drafts a letter that goes nowhere until you approve it.

How it is kept honest

  • The app's own code does the math, and the model only explains it.
  • Every number the model writes is checked against your real data.
  • Each answer names the model that wrote it.
  • One switch on the Privacy and data page turns it off.

05Why it matters

        
Affordable internet only counts if you get what you pay for.

      

    


    
Two targets of Goal 9 are about the internet. In a city like Huntsville most homes can already get a connection, so the gap is whether the speed people pay for is real.

    

      
Target 9.c
Universal and affordable access to the internet
In plain words, everyone should be able to get online at a price they can manage, and a plan is not affordable when part of the bill buys speed that never arrives.

      
Target 9.1
Reliable infrastructure with affordable, fair access
In plain words, the networks people rely on should work as promised and be fair to everyone who pays for them.
